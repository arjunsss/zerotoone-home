v0.2
who this is for

Everyone starts at commit zero.

Three kinds of people show up to zero-to-one labs. If you see yourself below, you're exactly who we're making this for.

role: career-switcher

As a stay-at-home parent who's never shipped a line of code, I want a way into tech that doesn't start with a $40k bootcamp, so I can prove to myself — and the group chat — that it's not too late.

role: big-tech-lifer

As a senior engineer with great equity and a slowly dimming soul, I want to see what leaving actually looks like, so I can decide if the standup that could've been an email is really worth staying for.

role: side-project-founder

As someone with a side project, twelve users, and a spreadsheet named "MAYBE THIS IS SOMETHING," I want to know how anyone actually reaches product-market fit, so I can stop refreshing analytics every four minutes.

v0.3
what we ship

Mostly short videos. Occasionally a receipts folder.

Six kinds of content, tagged the way we'd tag the commits, if any of this were a commit.

feat:

Build First, Think Later

Arjun and Anennya — two people with full-time tech jobs and zero home-improvement licenses — use AI to build real household projects. Physics gets involved. Return policies get tested. Nobody thinks first.

fix:

Teardowns

We open up real products — apps, gadgets, landing pages, pitch decks — and walk through what works, what doesn't, and what someone clearly shipped on a Friday at 6pm.

docs:

Founder Interviews

Conversations with people who actually made the jump to their own thing — why they started, what nearly made them quit, and the parts that never make it into the pitch deck.

chore:

Solo Explainers

Short, unglamorous videos where one of us explains a piece of the zero-to-one journey — badly at first, then slightly less badly.

README.md

Build Write-Ups

Longer breakdowns of what we've built, repo linked, so you can steal the homework and run your own version at home.

test:

Live Workshops

Livestreamed build sessions with real-time questions. No safety net, no dress rehearsal, frequent regrets.
